# Charter 003: Wayland data-control capture (T015)

Session: 2026-10-24, about 25 minutes. Tester: builder (charter track, wave 6). Code under test: `dev` at 6308b65.

## Mission

*Explore* Wayland data-control capture and serving *with* selection churn, slow and stalling sources, readers that close early or never drain, and compositor exit during serve *to discover* invariant violations: no blocked daemon, no partial entries, and recovery when the compositor returns.

## Setup

- Headless sway 1.10 in the tools image (`WLR_BACKENDS=headless`, pixman), with the daemon's `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` in sway's runtime dir.
- `wl-copy`/`wl-paste` for shell scenarios, and the `tests/support/wl_client.rs` client in an ad-hoc probe test (scratch copy of the repo) for stalling sources and multi-type offers.
- Synthetic text only.

## What I tried

1. **Churn.** 500 `wl-copy` in a tight loop (0.63 s), then 300 more while sampling `status` latency.
2. **Readers that close early.** A 1 MB entry restored with `rstory copy 0`, then read with `wl-paste | head -c 20`, ten more early closes, then one full read.
3. **Slow reader.** `wl-paste` into a pipe nobody drains for 15 s, while `status` runs.
4. **Compositor exit during serve.** SIGTERM to sway while a slow reader is mid-transfer. Then sway again on the same socket name, and a copy.
5. **Stalling sources.** Ten selections in a row whose text types never get written, then a normal copy.
6. **Type sets:** `text/plain` only, `text/uri-list` only, `image/png` only, empty text, exactly 1 MiB, 1 MiB + 1, and the hint cases (charter 005).

## Observations

- **Protocol.** The client bound `zwlr_data_control_manager_v1`; sway 1.10 does not offer `ext_data_control_manager_v1`.
- **Churn.** 500 copies were recorded in order, keeping the cap of 20, with `dropped_burst = 0`. `status` answered in 3 to 5 ms under churn.
- **Early-close readers.** Eleven early closes did not disturb the serve: a full read of 1 000 000 bytes took 4 ms, and the state stayed `recording`.
- **Slow reader.** A pipe that is never drained does not block the daemon: `status` answered in 4 to 5 ms.
- **Compositor exit.** The daemon went `inactive (disconnected)` and stayed alive. It reconnected on its own to the new sway and recorded the next copy.
- **Stalling sources.** Ten stalling sources never blocked `status` (2 to 3 ms). The normal copy after them was recorded within 234 ms of the first stall, because only the current offer is read.
- **Type sets.**
  - `text/plain` only is recorded.
  - `text/uri-list` only, `image/png` only and empty text are ignored.
  - 1 MiB is recorded; 1 MiB + 1 is skipped (`skipped_oversize`).
- **Logs.** At `RUST_LOG=trace` the log carried types and sizes only; 0 of 164 lines held synthetic text.

## Findings (by severity)

No defects found in this session.

### W1 Info

The `ext-data-control-v1` path is still unexercised against a real compositor: sway 1.10 offers only zwlr. The convergence note already says so. It needs a KDE or newer-wlroots check, which the manual matrix row T014 now covers on `dev`.

## Open questions

- Is `text/uri-list` (file copies from file managers) deliberately out of scope? It's ignored today with no counter.
- The 1 MiB serve budget is still asserted at 1 s, not 200 ms (a convergence note). A 1 MB read here took 4 ms locally, so the tighter budget looks attainable.
